The annual renewals checklist for your business in Qatar

Published 1 November 2026 · Basira for Transactions

One expired document can block other transactions. That is why we recommend a clear renewal schedule for every business. These are the main documents renewed regularly:

Business documents

  • Commercial registration. Commercial Registration Renewal ›
  • Commercial licence. Trade Licence Issuance or Renewal ›
  • Establishment card.
  • Commercial lease and its attestation. Commercial or Residential Lease Registration ›

Staff documents

  • Residence permits.
  • Health cards and food-handler health certificates. Health Certificate for Food Handlers ›
  • Attested employment contracts.

Practical tips

  • Start each renewal at least a month before the due date.
  • Settle violations before renewing, as some block the transaction.
  • Keep digital copies of every document in one file.
  • Make one person responsible for the schedule.
