Partners & referrals programme

Refer a client and earn 10% of our fees

Accountants, company-setup offices, restaurant suppliers and real-estate offices meet business owners first. If you refer a client who signs with us, you earn a 10% commission on the Basira for Transactions fees they pay.

Join as a partnerAsk on WhatsApp

How it works

  1. Sign up with the form below and receive your own referral code on WhatsApp.
  2. Refer the client by sending us their name and number, or the client mentions your code.
  3. We serve the client and invoice them as usual.
  4. Your commission is paid monthly with a clear statement.

Who can join

Accountants and audit firmsYour clients need renewals, item registration and licences.
Setup and real-estate officesEvery new business needs a series of transactions after setup.
Restaurant and equipment suppliersYour clients need menu registration and health certificates.
IndividualsIf you know a business owner who needs us, refer them.

Key terms

Commission10% of the Basira for Transactions fees actually collected from the referred client, excluding government fees and expenses.
PeriodOn all invoices collected in the first 12 months from the client's first invoice.
PaymentMonthly, after the invoice is collected, by bank transfer with a statement.
Valid referralCounted when your referral or code reaches us before the client signs, and the client was not already ours.
AgreementThe partnership is set out in a written agreement before the first payment; either side may end it by written notice.

Example: a client you referred signs for 20 items at QAR 2,000 in fees, so your commission is QAR 200 once collected.
