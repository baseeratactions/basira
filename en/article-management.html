When does your business need a monthly management contract?

Published 1 November 2026 · Basira for Transactions

Many restaurant owners and small businesses start by paying for each transaction separately. As the business grows, transactions and deadlines multiply, and the question becomes: is it better for one team to run the whole file for a fixed monthly fee?

Signs you need it

  • You have more than one branch, or a menu that changes often.
  • You have missed a renewal at least once and paid a fine.
  • You have no full-time PRO.
  • You or your manager spend hours every week on government visits.
  • You are an investor who does not live in Qatar full time.

What the contract should cover

  • A clear list of the transactions included, and what is charged separately.
  • A schedule of every renewal date with early warning.
  • One account manager and a fast way to reach them.
  • A regular report of what was done and what is next.
  • Government fees kept separate from fees, with a receipt for each.

Compared with paying per transaction

Paying per transaction suits very small businesses or rare transactions. When transactions are many and recurring, a fixed monthly amount gives you predictable costs and lowers the risk of missed deadlines and fines.
