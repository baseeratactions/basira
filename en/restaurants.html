Questions restaurant owners ask

Does every menu item need to be registered?

The Ministry of Commerce and Industry requires items and their prices to be registered before they are sold, and malls and delivery apps ask for it too. We review your menu with you and confirm what needs registering before we start.

What if I have more than 5 branches?

The base price includes 5 sales outlets per item; each extra outlet adds QAR 10 per item. The calculator above does this for you.

Which documents are needed?

Commercial registration and establishment card, the item list with name, description and sales unit, a clear photo of each item, its selling price, and the list of branches and outlets.

Are government fees included?

No. Our fees are separate, and any government fee is paid at its official amount against a receipt in your business's name.
