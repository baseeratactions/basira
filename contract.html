Clause 7: Payment terms

  2. Second payment of 25% when the applications are submitted to the authorities or half the work is completed, whichever is earlier.
  3. Final payment of 25% on completion of the work and before final certificates and documents are handed over.

Each payment is due within seven (7) days of notice, by bank transfer to the Company's account, by cheque in its name, or in cash against its official receipt. Payments handed to any employee personally are not valid. The Company may retain issued certificates and documents until all amounts due are paid, in accordance with the Qatari Civil Code.
